FAQ

What if I don't like the first cut?
You get revisions included with every package — I keep refining until you're completely happy. No extra charges within your package's scope.
How do I send my raw footage?
After ordering, I'll send you a Google Drive or WeTransfer link to upload your files securely — usually within 24 hours.
What format should my raw footage be in?
Any format works — MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV. The higher the quality of the source, the better the final result.
What format will I receive?
All common sizes: 16:9 (YouTube), 9:16 (TikTok/Reels), 1:1 (Instagram), 4:5 (feed). ProRes masters available for further editing too.
Can I use the edited video commercially?
Yes — all deliverables are yours to use on any platform, commercially, with no extra licensing fees.
What's Kinetic Sync Editing™?
It's my signature style — cinematic zoom-ins, seamless scene transitions and audio-synced cuts that make your video flow like a film instead of a slideshow.
Can you work with my specific software?
Yes. I work in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and After Effects, and can export project-ready files for any NLE (Final Cut, Vegas, etc).
